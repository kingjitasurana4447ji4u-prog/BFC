About BFC

The World's Premier MMA Promotion

The BFC (Brutal Fighting Championship) is the world's premier mixed martial arts promotion company, producing over 40 live events annually and managing a roster of over 578 elite fighters across 11 weight divisions.

As a subsidiary of TKO Group Holdings, BFC organizes, promotes, and broadcasts fighting events globally, utilizing its signature fenced-in Octagon — the most iconic stage in combat sports.

The Arena

The Octagon

The iconic fenced-in battleground where every BFC fight takes place — engineered for maximum action.

OCTAGON
📐

30-Foot Diameter

The Octagon spans 30 feet across and 750 square feet of fighting surface — the perfect arena for elite MMA combat.

🔗

Chain-Link Fencing

6-foot high vinyl-coated chain-link fence with padded posts ensures fighter safety while keeping the action contained.

🛡️

Canvas & Padding

EVA foam padded flooring beneath a stretched canvas surface provides the ideal balance of grip and impact absorption.

🚪

Two Entry Gates

Opposing entry gates allow fighters to enter from separate sides, building anticipation for each epic showdown.
